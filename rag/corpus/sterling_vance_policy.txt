SECTION 1 - OVERVIEW
Sterling Vance Bank Dispute & Chargeback Operations Manual v2.4
Effective Date: January 1, 2026

SECTION 2 - DEFINITIONS
Define: Chargeback, Dispute, Reason Code, Refund Window, Escalation, Authorization, High-Risk Merchant
Define 'unauthorized transaction' as: 'A transaction initiated without the explicit consent or authorization of the account holder, including transactions resulting from account compromise, card skimming, phishing, or identity theft.'

SECTION 3 - ANALYST ROLES AND AUTHORIZATION
3.1 Junior Analyst: May approve refunds up to $500.00. Cannot escalate to card network. Cannot override fraud flags.
3.2 Senior Analyst: May approve refunds of any amount. Only role authorized to formally escalate disputes to card networks. Must countersign all refunds exceeding $1,000.00.
3.3 Section 3 sign-off: Required for all refund denials affecting amounts over $1,000.00.
3.4 Section 9 sign-off (Senior Supervisor): Required jointly with Section 3 for refund denials over $1,000.00. Both signatures required within 72 hours.

SECTION 4 - REASON CODES
4.1 General: Reason codes follow VISA and Mastercard international frameworks.
4.2 Specific codes:
- Code 4853: Services Not Rendered or Merchandise Not Received. Refund window: 120 days from transaction date. Customer must provide proof of expected delivery date.
- Code 4855: Non-receipt of Merchandise. Refund window: 90 days. Tracking confirmation required.
- Code 4863: Cardholder Does Not Recognize Transaction. Refund window: 60 days. Requires fraud investigation before refund.
- Code 4837: No Cardholder Authorization. Refund window: 120 days. Triggers automatic fraud flag.
4.3 Chargeback thresholds defined in Rule 4.2b: A merchant is placed on the Chargeback Monitoring Program when their chargeback ratio exceeds 1.0% of total monthly transactions OR when absolute chargeback count exceeds 100 per month, whichever occurs first.

SECTION 5 - REFUND ELIGIBILITY WINDOWS
5.1 Standard refund windows by dispute type:
- Duplicate charge disputes: 90 days from original charge date. Customer must provide both transaction receipts.
- Unauthorized transaction disputes: 120 days from statement date. Automatic if fraud score > 80.
- Service quality disputes: 60 days from service date.
- Subscription cancellation disputes: 30 days from last billing date.
5.2 Expedited processing: Disputes involving amounts over $500 AND fraud risk score > 75 qualify for expedited 5-business-day processing.

SECTION 6 - FRAUD RISK SCORING
6.1 Sterling Vance uses a 0-100 fraud risk score updated by the risk engine.
6.2 Score thresholds:
- 0-39: Low risk. Routine processing.
- 40-74: Medium risk. Enhanced verification required.
- 75-100: High risk. Automatic escalation trigger. Senior analyst required.
6.3 Impact on escalation routing: A dispute with fraud risk score >= 75 automatically routes to the escalation queue regardless of dispute amount. The escalation flag appears in the analyst dashboard and the MCP tool 'escalate_dispute' becomes available.
6.4 Merchant fraud risk: Merchants with a risk_score >= 80 are classified as high-risk merchants. Disputes involving high-risk merchants require senior analyst review for any amount.

SECTION 7 - ESCALATION PROCEDURES
7.1 Escalation triggers: amount > $500 OR customer risk_flag = 'high' OR merchant risk_score >= 80.
7.2 Escalation steps for $750 fraud disputes with high-risk merchants:
7.2.1 Step 1: Senior analyst reviews all transaction evidence within 24 hours of escalation flag.
7.2.2 Step 2: Fraud investigation report must be attached to the dispute record.
7.2.3 Step 3: Merchant chargeback history retrieved and documented.
7.2.4 Step 4: If merchant has >= 3 prior chargebacks in 90 days, submit to Chargeback Monitoring Program.
7.2.5 Step 5: Card network formal notification submitted within 45 days of original transaction.
7.3 Required documentation for escalated disputes: (a) Original transaction record, (b) Customer dispute form, (c) Fraud investigation report, (d) Merchant response if solicited within 10 days, (e) Senior analyst sign-off form.

SECTION 8 - VISA AND MASTERCARD RULES
8.1 VISA Rule 10.4: Applies to disputes where the cardholder claims non-receipt of cash from an ATM. Investigation must be completed within 30 days. Provisional credit issued within 5 business days.
8.2 VISA Rule 10.5: Applies to disputes involving counterfeit card fraud at point-of-sale terminals. Chip liability shift applies: if merchant did not have EMV-capable terminal, liability shifts to merchant. Investigation window: 120 days.
8.3 Mastercard Rule 4531: Duplicate processing disputes. Both original and duplicate transaction must be evidenced. Refund window: 45 days.

SECTION 9 - MULTI-CONDITION DISPUTE HANDLING
9.1 When a junior analyst flags a dispute AND the merchant has prior chargebacks AND the amount exceeds $500: The dispute is automatically escalated to senior analyst queue. Junior analyst cannot approve or deny. Must document initial findings in dispute notes field.
9.2 Disputes involving both duplicate charge and merchant fraud (compound disputes): Senior analyst must complete the following sequence: (1) Verify duplicate charge independently, (2) Run merchant fraud history check, (3) Determine which policy applies (duplicate charge or fraud), applying the stricter standard, (4) If fraud confirmed: apply Code 4837 rules, (5) If duplicate only: apply Code 4853 or Mastercard 4531.
9.3 Section 9 sign-off requirement: For any refund denial involving amounts over $1,000.00, Section 9 (Senior Supervisor) sign-off is required jointly with Section 3 authorization. This dual sign-off must be completed within 72 hours of denial decision.

SECTION 10 - RECORD RETENTION
10.1 All dispute records retained 7 years.
10.2 Fraud investigation reports retained 10 years.
10.3 Escalation records retained 5 years.

SECTION 11 - COMPLIANCE
11.1 All dispute decisions must comply with Regulation E (Electronic Fund Transfer Act).
11.2 FCRA requirements for credit-related disputes.
11.3 PCI-DSS for card data handling during investigations.
